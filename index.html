FAQ

Does it work with both ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes. ChatVault supports both ChatGPT (Free, Plus, Team) and Claude (claude.ai). You can export from both in the same app, and MemoryVault works across both sets of conversations.

What is MemoryVault and how does it work?

MemoryVault analyzes your backed-up conversations using an AI model (via your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key) and distills them into a portable memory profile. It generates three output files: one for ChatGPT Custom Instructions, one for Claude Project instructions, and a generic system prompt for any AI. The analysis typically costs under $0.50 in API credits.

macOS says it can't verify the app — is it safe?

Yes. Because ChatVault isn't yet registered with Apple's notarization program, macOS shows a warning on first launch. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway". This is a one-time step.

Is my data private?

Completely. ChatVault runs entirely on your computer. Your conversations are downloaded directly from ChatGPT/Claude to your local folder — they never touch our servers. For MemoryVault, your conversation text is sent to OpenAI or Anthropic via your own API key (the same way ChatGPT itself works).

Does it work with ChatGPT Team or Business?

Yes. ChatVault automatically detects your account type and handles Team and Business exports correctly.

My account has 1000+ conversations — will it still work?

Yes. ChatVault is built for large accounts. It handles rate limiting automatically, resumes if interrupted, and never re-downloads what's already saved. A 1000-conversation export typically takes 20–40 minutes in the background.

What is the refund policy?

If ChatVault doesn't work on your machine, reach out within 30 days for a full refund. No questions asked.
